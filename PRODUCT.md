## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, roughly equal in priority:

- **Prospective buyers** — people curious about the Feel Great System who land on the site to decide whether to buy Unimate and Balance for themselves.
- **Prospective distributors** — people considering joining Unicity as a distributor under Lina, drawn in by her personal transformation story and the business opportunity.

Both groups typically arrive via Lina's personal/social channels (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook — @oueisbeauty) or word of mouth, already primed by her story rather than cold search traffic.

## Product Purpose

The site exists to convert visitors into either a product purchase or a distributor sign-up for the Feel Great System (Unimate + Balance), using Lina Oueis's personal testimonial and a simple quick-start guide as the persuasive core. Success is a click-through to Unicity's own purchase flow (ufeelgreat.com) or enrollment flow (enrollment.unicity.com), or direct contact with Lina.

## Positioning

Not a general Unicity storefront — a single distributor's personal-story-driven referral site. The mechanism is Lina's own before/after journey (fatigue, bloating, weight) plus a dead-simple 3-step protocol (Unimate in the morning, Balance before meals, no diet overhaul), not product comparison or clinical argument.

## Product Principles

1. Every path on the site leads to one of two outbound actions: buy, or enroll as a distributor — nothing should dead-end without a next step.
2. Lina's personal story is the trust anchor; the site should read as one real person's account, not a corporate product page.
3. Respect the independent-distributor boundary: never present the site as Unicity's official voice, and keep the required disclaimer intact and visible.
4. Keep the buy/enroll mechanics off-site and frictionless — the site's job is to persuade and hand off, not to replicate Unicity's commerce flows.
5. Treat health-outcome claims (Lina's own and the Trustpilot quotes) as real evidence to be quoted faithfully, not marketing copy to be embellished.

Feel Great Quick Start Guide

Exactly what to take, and when, each day.

  • Drink one serving of Unimate first thing in the morning on an empty stomach.
  • Supports energy, mental focus, and metabolism.
  • Mix with 500-750ml of water for the best results.

  • About 4 hours after Unimate (around 12:00), drink one serving of Balance 10 minutes before your first main meal.
  • Supports healthy digestion, blood sugar levels already within a normal range, and helps manage cravings.
  • Mix with 250ml of water, shake well, and drink immediately.

  • After dinner, wait about 16 hours before your next meal.
  • This gives your body time to rest before the next day begins.

Unicity Balance - How it Works

Feel Great Quick Start Guide

This is a practical step-by-step guide: exactly what to do, and when, each day.

  • Drink one serving of Unimate in the morning on an empty stomach.
  • Helps boost energy, mental clarity, and mood while supporting metabolism.
  • Mix with 500-750ml of water for the best results.

  • Drink one serving of Balance 10 minutes before each of your two largest meals of the day.
  • Supports healthy digestion, blood sugar levels, and reduces cravings.
  • Mix with 250ml of water, shake well, and drink immediately.

  • No need for strict diets, just maintain a balanced lifestyle.
  • Listen to your body's needs as you go through your day.

  • After dinner, avoid eating again for about 16 hours to continue the fasting period.
  • This gives your body time to rest before your next meal.

Your Daily Flow

  1. Morning Unimate Energy, focus, and metabolism support.
  2. Before Meals Balance Digestion, blood sugar balance, and fewer cravings.
  3. Evening Normal Meals Maintain a balanced lifestyle.
  4. Overnight Intermittent Fasting Approximately 16 hours before your next meal.
